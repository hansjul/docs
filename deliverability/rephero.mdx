RepHero is the proactive email reputation management service in the mySMTP platform. It combines automated lookups on 100+ public blacklists and various private blacklists with human consultants who help you fix problems.

## How RepHero works

- **Automated monitoring:** your sending domains and IPs are checked continuously, and you get live notifications when something changes.
- **Human consultants:** nearly all blacklists, public and private, need human interaction to resolve a listing. mySMTP consultants handle delisting and advise on best practices.
- **Deep industry knowledge:** mySMTP keeps good relations with ISPs and free email provider support teams, and decodes soft and hard bounce messages.

<Warning>
  RepHero is not a magic bullet. You still need opt-in, cleaned data and industry best practices to keep a good reputation.
</Warning>
