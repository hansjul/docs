## SPF (Sender Policy Framework)

SPF is like a guest list. It tells the internet which servers are allowed to send emails from your domain.

### DNS record

Add a TXT record to your domain's DNS:

```text
v=spf1 include:spf1.mysmtp.com ~all
```

This tells receiving servers that mySMTP is authorized to send email for your domain.

<Note>
  If your domain already has an SPF record, add `include:spf1.mysmtp.com` to the existing record instead of publishing a second one. Only one SPF record is allowed per domain.
</Note>

### How to verify

Use the mySMTP [SPF Validator](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/) to expand every `include:`, count DNS lookups (limit is 10), and catch syntax errors or weak endings like `~all` or `?all`.

## DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)

DKIM puts a digital signature on your emails so recipients know they have not been altered in transit.

### DNS record

mySMTP gives you a DKIM record for your account. Add it as a TXT record:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | TXT |
| Name | `mysmtp._domainkey` (or the selector provided in your setup email) |
| Value | The 2048-bit public key generated for your account |

### How to verify

Use the mySMTP [DKIM Checker](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/) to verify the domain, selector, key type, and key length. The tool warns if the key is under 1024 bits.

## DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication)

DMARC tells email services what to do when SPF or DKIM fail, and it sends you reports so you can spot abuse.

### DNS record

Add a TXT record to your DNS:

```text
v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com
```

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `v=DMARC1` | Required version identifier |
| `p=none` | Policy: monitor only (do not block yet) |
| `rua=` | Aggregate report delivery address |

### Recommended rollout

Start with `p=none` to collect data without rejecting mail. After 2 to 4 weeks of clean reports, move to `p=quarantine` to send failing mail to spam. Once you are confident, move to `p=reject` to block spoofed messages entirely.

## BIMI (Brand Indicators for Message Identification)

BIMI displays your brand logo next to your emails in supporting inboxes, such as Gmail on mobile and web.

### Requirements

- DMARC must be in place with `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`
- A verified Mark Certificate (VMC) or Common Mark Certificate (CMC) is required for Gmail
- Your logo must be in SVG Tiny PS format

### DNS record

Add a TXT record:

```text
v=BIMI1; l=https://yourdomain.com/logo.svg;
```

### Tools

- Convert your logo to SVG Tiny PS with the mySMTP [BIMI SVG Converter](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/)
- Generate the full BIMI TXT record with the mySMTP [BIMI Generator](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/)

<Note>
  BIMI is not supported by every mailbox provider. Even where it is supported, the logo may not appear if DMARC alignment fails or the certificate requirement is not met.
</Note>
