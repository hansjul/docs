## If you are blacklisted

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the cause">
    Review recent sending activity in your mySMTP dashboard. Look for:

    - Sudden volume spikes
    - High bounce rates
    - Spam complaints
    - Compromised credentials or unauthorized sending
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the root problem">
    Remove bad addresses from your list, secure any compromised accounts, and pause campaigns until the issue is resolved. Continuing to send from a blacklisted IP deepens the reputation damage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request removal">
    Visit the blacklist operator's website and follow their delisting process. Most require a self-service form. Some remove listings automatically after a period of good behavior. Be honest in your request and explain the steps you took to fix the cause.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify delivery">
    After delisting, send test messages to addresses at Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, and Apple iCloud. Confirm that messages arrive in the inbox and not in spam.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prevent future listings

- Keep bounce rates under 2% and spam complaint rates under 0.1%.
- Use confirmed opt-in and remove non-engagers.
- Authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. See [DNS setup for authentication](/deliverability/dns-setup-for-authentication).
- Warm up new dedicated IPs gradually. See [Warm up plans](/deliverability/warm-up-plans).
- Send consistent volumes rather than sudden spikes.

## Need help?

If your IP remains listed after you have fixed the cause and requested removal, [contact mySMTP support](/support/contact-support) with your account email, the blacklist name, and any error messages you received.
