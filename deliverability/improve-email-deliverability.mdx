## Authentication setup

Authenticate every domain you send from. ISPs use these records to verify you are a legitimate sender.

| Record | Purpose | Quick setup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SPF | Lists authorized sending servers | `v=spf1 include:spf1.mysmtp.com ~all` |
| DKIM | Cryptographically signs each message | Add the TXT record mySMTP provides for `mysmtp._domainkey` |
| DMARC | Tells receivers how to handle auth failures | Start with `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com` |

For step-by-step DNS instructions, see [DNS setup for authentication](/deliverability/dns-setup-for-authentication) and [SPF, DKIM, DMARC and BIMI explained](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc-and-bimi-explained).

## Major provider requirements (2025-2026)

Gmail, Yahoo, and Microsoft enforce stricter sender standards. Meeting these protects your deliverability across all providers.

| Requirement | Gmail / Yahoo | Microsoft (Outlook.com) | How to satisfy it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SPF | Required | Required | Publish an SPF record that includes mySMTP |
| DKIM | Required | Required | Publish the DKIM record from your mySMTP setup email |
| DMARC | Required | Recommended | Publish a DMARC record; plan to move from `p=none` to `p=quarantine` then `p=reject` |
| One-click unsubscribe | Required for bulk | Recommended | Include `List-Unsubscribe` with RFC 8058 functionality |
| Spam complaint rate | Under 0.3% | Under 0.3% | Remove complainers immediately; send only to engaged recipients |
| Valid reverse DNS | Required | Required | Included with dedicated IPs |
| TLS encryption | Required | Required | mySMTP uses STARTTLS on port 587 |

<Note>
  Bulk senders are typically those sending more than 5,000 messages per day to Gmail or Yahoo addresses.
</Note>

## IP strategy

Choose the IP setup that matches your volume and control needs.

| Factor | Shared IP (DK only) | Dedicated IP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Under 100,000 emails per month, inconsistent volume | Over 100,000 emails per month, consistent sending |
| Reputation control | Managed by mySMTP | Fully yours |
| Warmup required | No | Yes, 21 to 45 days |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |

See [IP reputation: dedicated vs. shared IPs](/deliverability/ip-reputation-dedicated-vs-shared-ips) for details.

## List hygiene

- Remove hard bounces immediately. mySMTP suppresses them automatically.
- Use confirmed (double) opt-in to ensure subscribers want your mail.
- Segment by engagement and remove addresses that have not opened or clicked in 6 to 12 months.
- Keep bounce rates under 2% and spam complaint rates under 0.1%.

## Content and sending practices

- Keep a healthy text-to-image ratio. Avoid image-only emails.
- Use a recognizable from name and from address.
- Include a clear unsubscribe link in every marketing message.
- Avoid spam trigger phrases and excessive punctuation.
- Send on a consistent schedule. Sudden volume spikes damage reputation.

## Monitoring

- Check [Microsoft SNDS](https://postmaster.live.com/) for Outlook.com IP reputation and spam trap data.
