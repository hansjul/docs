The right choice depends on your send volume, budget, and how much control you want over sender reputation.

<Note>
  Shared IP plans are only available to customers in Denmark.
</Note>

## Plan comparison

| Capability | Shared IP (DK only) | Dedicated IP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Reputation control | Limited (depends on the pool) | Full (only your traffic) |
| Warm-up required | No | Yes |
| Ready to send | Immediately | After setup (credentials sent within 2 hours during business hours) |
| Dashboard and user management | Not available | Available at app.mysmtp.com |
| Best for | Low-to-moderate volume, quick start | High volume, marketing, transactional, full deliverability control |

## Shared IP (DK only)

A shared IP is used by multiple mySMTP customers. The pool already has an established reputation, so you can start sending right away. mySMTP monitors the pool to keep deliverability high.

The trade-off is that poor practices by another sender in the pool can affect your delivery. Shared IPs are best if you send moderate volumes and want a simple, managed solution.

## Dedicated IP

A dedicated IP is reserved for your account. Since you are the only sender, your reputation depends entirely on your own practices. This gives you full control, but it also means you must warm up the IP by gradually increasing volume over days or weeks.

Dedicated IPs are best for high-volume senders or anyone who wants precise control over deliverability and the option to monitor sending from the dashboard.

## How to choose

- Choose a **shared IP (DK only)** if you send lower volumes, need a quick start, and prefer a managed reputation.
- Choose a **dedicated IP** if you send large volumes regularly and want full control over your sender reputation.
