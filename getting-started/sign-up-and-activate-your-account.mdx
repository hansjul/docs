The process is fully automated for shared IP plans and handled with a short setup window for dedicated IP plans.

## Sign up for a dedicated IP

mySMTP offers monthly recurring packages based on the number of emails you need to send each month. Visit the recurring email order page to view available packages and place your order.

You can pay by **Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Crypto**.

<Tip>
  No company credit card? Use the [manual account setup form](/billing/manual-account-setup) and pay by bank transfer.
</Tip>

If you pay by **Credit Card**, the card is stored securely and charged automatically every 30 days. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your [customer dashboard](https://clients.mysmtp.com).

Once we receive your order, we will set up your dedicated IP server and send a **setup email** with your login details and DNS configuration instructions. During business hours (9 AM to 5 PM, GMT+1) on weekdays, setup details are sent within **2 hours**.

Your server is active as soon as you receive the setup email.

## Sign up for a shared IP (DK only)

Shared IP subscriptions are fully automated. When you **sign up and complete payment** by **Credit Card**, you receive an **invoice receipt** and a **setup email** immediately. Your SMTP server is active right away.

Follow the setup instructions in the email to configure your email client or application and add the recommended DNS records.

<Note>
  Shared IP plans are only available to customers in Denmark.
</Note>
