## Required DNS records

| Record type | Name / Host | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TXT | @ or your apex domain | `v=spf1 include:spf1.mysmtp.com ~all` |
| TXT | `mysmtp._domainkey` | The 2048-bit DKIM key from your mySMTP setup |
| TXT | `_dmarc` | `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com` |

<Note>
  Use the exact DKIM record value from your mySMTP setup email. Do not use a placeholder key in production.
</Note>

## How to add the records

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your DNS provider">
    Go to the control panel for your domain registrar or DNS host (for example, GoDaddy, Namecheap, or One.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the DNS records section">
    Look for **DNS Settings**, **Manage DNS**, or **DNS Records**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or update the SPF record">
    If your domain already has a TXT record for SPF, add `include:spf1.mysmtp.com` to it. There should only be one SPF record per domain. Do not publish a second SPF record.

    Example single record:

    ```text
    v=spf1 include:spf1.mysmtp.com ~all
    ```

    Example combined with an existing provider:

    ```text
    v=spf1 include:spf1.mysmtp.com include:_spf.google.com ~all
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DKIM record">
    Create a new TXT record with the name `mysmtp._domainkey` and paste the DKIM key value from your mySMTP setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DMARC record">
    Create a new TXT record with the name `_dmarc`. Start with `p=none` so you can monitor results without impacting delivery. You can move to `p=quarantine` and later `p=reject` once you are confident your setup is correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for DNS propagation">
    DNS changes usually take effect within minutes, but full propagation can take up to 24 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify your setup

After propagation, use [test.smtp.ai](https://test.smtp.ai) to check that SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are passing. You can also review the **DNS verifications** section in the mySMTP dashboard after the records have had time to update.

## DMARC rollout guidelines

For 2026 sender requirements from Gmail, Yahoo, and Outlook, you need SPF and DKIM alignment and a valid DMARC record. Start with `p=none`, review aggregate reports, then move to `p=quarantine` and finally `p=reject` once you confirm legitimate mail is authenticating correctly.
