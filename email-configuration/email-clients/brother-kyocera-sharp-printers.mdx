## SMTP settings

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Server | `relayxx.mysmtp.com` (from your setup email) |
| Server (shared IP, DK only) | `mail.mysmtp.com` |
| Port | **587** |
| Connection encryption | **STARTTLS** |
| Authentication | Enabled |
| User Name | Your mySMTP SMTP username |
| Password | Your mySMTP password |

<Note>
  Use the exact relay hostname from your mySMTP setup email. `relayxx.mysmtp.com` is only an example.
</Note>

<Note>
  Shared IP plans are only available to customers in Denmark. All other customers use a dedicated hostname.
</Note>

## Setup by brand

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Brother">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Web Based Management">
        Open a web browser, enter the printer's IP address, and log in as an administrator.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Navigate to SMTP settings">
        Go to **Network** > **Protocol** > **SMTP Advanced Settings**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the SMTP server">
        In **SMTP Server**, enter your mySMTP relay hostname (e.g. `relayxx.mysmtp.com`).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the port and encryption">
        Set **SMTP Port** to **587** and set **SSL/TLS** or **Security** to **STARTTLS**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enable authentication">
        Turn on **SMTP Authentication** and enter your mySMTP **User Name** and **Password**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the sender address">
        Enter the **Sender E-mail Address** (for example, `printer@yourdomain.com`). This domain must be on your mySMTP account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save and test">
        Click **Submit** or **Apply**. Send a test scan-to-email to confirm delivery.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

## Troubleshooting

- **Cannot connect:** check that your firewall allows outbound traffic on port 587. If blocked, try port **2525** or **25**.
- **Authentication failed:** re-enter your mySMTP username and password. They are case-sensitive.
- **Relay denied:** the sender address must use a domain authenticated for your mySMTP account. See [DNS settings quick explainer](/getting-started/dns-settings-quick-explainer).
- **No STARTTLS option:** on older models, choose **TLS** or enable the highest available encryption setting and confirm the port is 587.

For general scan-to-email guidance, see [Scan to email](/email-configuration/email-clients/scan-to-email). For error codes, see [Common SMTP errors](/troubleshooting/common-smtp-errors).
