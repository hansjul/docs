## SMTP settings

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Outgoing mail server | `relayxx.mysmtp.com` (from your setup email) |
| Outgoing mail server (shared IP, DK only) | `mail.mysmtp.com` |
| Port | **587** |
| Encryption | **STARTTLS** |
| Authentication | Required |
| Username / password | Your mySMTP SMTP username and password |

<Note>
  Shared IP plans are only available to customers in Denmark.
</Note>

## Configure Outlook

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Outlook 2025 (beta)">
    The desktop client Outlook 2025 has not yet been announced final, so this is the guide for those testing the BETA version.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open account settings">
        Open Outlook, click the **Settings** icon, and then click **Manage** for the email account you want to use with mySMTP.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Unlock account editing">
        Type the **Password** for incoming email to enable account editing.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the SMTP settings">
        In the outgoing (SMTP) section, enter the server, port, encryption, username, and password from the table above.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Click **Continue** and wait for confirmation.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Outlook 2016 to 2025">
    <Note>
      Your account already uses IMAP for incoming mail. Follow the steps below to configure the outgoing SMTP server.
    </Note>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open account settings">
        Go to **File** > **Account Settings** > **Account Settings...**
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select your account">
        Select your existing email account **(IMAP)** and click **Change...**
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the outgoing server">
        Set **Outgoing mail server** to your mySMTP server, then click **More Settings...**
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn on authentication">
        On the **Outgoing Server** tab, check **My outgoing server (SMTP) requires authentication**, then choose **Log on using** and enter your mySMTP username and password.

        If your mySMTP login is the same as your incoming mail login, you can choose **Use same settings as my incoming mail server** instead.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set port and encryption">
        On the **Advanced** tab, set **Outgoing server (SMTP)** to **587** and **Use the following type of encrypted connection** to **STARTTLS**. Click **OK**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Test and finish">
        Click **Next** to test your settings. When the test succeeds, click **Close**, then **Finish**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Troubleshooting

- **Test fails on the outgoing server:** check that the username and password are your mySMTP SMTP credentials, not your mailbox login.
- **Connection times out:** your network may block port 587. Try port **2525**.
- **Relay denied:** make sure the sender address uses a domain authenticated for your mySMTP account. See [DNS settings quick explainer](/getting-started/dns-settings-quick-explainer).

For more error codes, see [Common SMTP errors](/troubleshooting/common-smtp-errors).
