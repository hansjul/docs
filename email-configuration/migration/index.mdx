Switching SMTP providers can affect deliverability if domain authentication and IP reputation are not handled carefully. This guide walks you through a safe cutover so your messages keep landing in the inbox.

## Before you start

- Keep your old provider active until cutover is complete and stable. Removing it too early can leave you without a working sending path.
- If you use a dedicated IP with mySMTP (the default plan), plan to warm it up gradually. Sending full volume from a cold IP can trigger blocks.
- If you use a shared IP plan (DK only), you can send at normal volume once DNS is verified.

## Migration checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and get credentials">
    Create your account at [clients.mysmtp.com](https://clients.mysmtp.com), complete activation, and note your SMTP hostname, username, and password from the setup email. See [Sign up and activate your account](/getting-started/sign-up-and-activate-your-account) and [Setting up SMTP credentials](/getting-started/setting-up-smtp-credentials).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add SPF and DKIM for mySMTP">
    Add `include:spf1.mysmtp.com` to your domain SPF record. A new record looks like `v=spf1 include:spf1.mysmtp.com ~all`; during migration, keep the old provider's include in the same record. Add mySMTP DKIM TXT/CNAME records as shown in your dashboard or setup email. Keep your old provider's records in place for now. See [DNS setup for authentication](/deliverability/dns-setup-for-authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your application">
    Update the SMTP hostname, port, and credentials in your apps, CMS, or code. Use the hostname from your setup email (for example `relayxx.mysmtp.com`) on port `587` with STARTTLS. See [Supported SMTP ports and encryption](/getting-started/supported-smtp-ports-and-encryption).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Warm up your dedicated IP gradually">
    Start with a small daily volume and increase slowly over days or weeks. This builds sender reputation on your new IP. See [Warm up plans](/deliverability/warm-up-plans) and use the [warm-up generator](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/#warmup-generator) in the mySMTP Deliverability Toolbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor bounces, complaints, and delivery">
    Watch your sending dashboard and inbox placement during the transition. Use the [Sender Readiness Checklist](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/) and [Email Header Analyzer](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/) in the mySMTP Deliverability Toolbox to verify authentication and headers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove old provider DNS records last">
    Only after sending is stable and reputation metrics look good, remove the old provider's SPF include and DKIM CNAME/TXT records. Updating DNS too early can break authentication if you need to roll back.
  </Step>
</Steps>
