Limits vary by plan, IP type, destination domain, and sending history.

## How limits are determined

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | Higher-tier plans include larger daily or monthly allowances |
| IP type | Dedicated IPs have higher limits than shared IPs (shared IPs are DK only) |
| Sending volume | Sudden spikes can trigger temporary throttling |
| Recipient domain | Each provider enforces its own hourly and daily caps |
| Bounce and spam rate | High rates lead to automatic rate reductions or blocks |

<Warning>
  Exceeding limits may result in temporary throttling, deferrals, or blocking. mySMTP monitors bounce and complaint rates automatically.
</Warning>

## IP warming strategy

New IPs have no reputation. Sending a large volume immediately looks suspicious to Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, and other providers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start small">
    Send to your most engaged recipients first. Users who open and click build positive reputation signals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Increase volume gradually">
    Double or triple your daily volume every few days rather than jumping to your full quota.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor bounces and complaints">
    Remove invalid addresses and suppress users who mark spam. Both metrics should stay below 2% and 0.3% respectively.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate every message">
    Use SPF, DKIM, and DMARC on your sending domain. See [DNS setup for authentication](/deliverability/dns-setup-for-authentication).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the free mySMTP [Warmup Plan Generator](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/#warmup-generator) to build a day-by-day schedule for a new IP or domain, and check your IP against major blocklists with the [Blacklist Checker](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/).
</Tip>

## Rate limits by destination provider

These are estimates based on current industry practice. Actual limits change and depend on your IP reputation.

| Provider | New IP (per hour) | Warmed-up IP (per hour) | Approximate daily cap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gmail | 500–1,000 | 5,000+ | 50,000 |
| Yahoo / AOL | 1,000 | 10,000+ | 100,000 |
| Outlook / Hotmail | 500 | 3,000+ | 30,000 |
| iCloud Mail | 500 | 2,000+ | 10,000 |

<Note>
  Separate your email streams by type and domain. For example, send transactional mail from `notifications.yourdomain.com` and marketing mail from `marketing.yourdomain.com`. This protects the reputation of each stream.
</Note>

## Bulk sender requirements (2025)

Gmail, Yahoo, and Microsoft now enforce minimum standards for bulk senders. Meeting them improves delivery through mySMTP.

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Authentication | Both SPF and DKIM required for bulk senders; DMARC at `p=none` minimum |
| Alignment | From domain must align with SPF or DKIM |
| TLS | All messages must be sent over TLS |
| One-click unsubscribe | Marketing messages need RFC 8058 List-Unsubscribe headers |
| Spam rate | Keep user-reported spam below 0.3%; ideally below 0.1% |
| Unsubscribe latency | Honor unsubscribe requests within 48 hours |

<Warning>
  Bulk senders who spoof gmail.com or send without TLS will receive temporary or permanent failures and may lose access to Gmail mitigation support.
</Warning>

## Throttling and error codes

When you hit a limit, the SMTP response tells you what happened.

| Error | Meaning | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 421 | Too many connections | Lower connection count; retry with backoff |
| 450 | Mailbox unavailable | Retry later; may be a temporary provider issue |
| 451 | Local server overload | Slow down sending rate |
| 550 | Blocked or rejected | Remove bad addresses; check reputation |
| 554 | Marked as spam | Review content and authentication setup |

Enable SMTP logging in your application to capture these codes. If you see repeated blocks, pause sending and review your list hygiene and authentication setup.

## Scaling to enterprise volume

If you need to send one million or more emails per day, consider:

- Multiple mySMTP dedicated IPs rotated for load distribution
- Postmulti server setups to spread traffic across relays
- Segregated sending domains for each mail stream
- Engagement-based sending strategies that reduce volume to inactive subscribers

<Note>
  Enterprise users can request custom rate limits and priority SMTP queues through mySMTP support.
</Note>
