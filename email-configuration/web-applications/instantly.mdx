You need an active mySMTP account with a dedicated IP and your SMTP credentials.

## Before you start

Have these details from your mySMTP setup email ready:

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| SMTP host | e.g. `relayxx.mysmtp.com` (from your setup email) |
| Port | `587` |
| Encryption | `TLS` |
| Username | Your mySMTP username |
| Password | Your mySMTP password |

<Note>
  Use the exact relay hostname from your setup email. `relayxx.mysmtp.com` is only an example.
</Note>

## Add mySMTP to Instantly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email Accounts">
    Sign in to [https://app.instantly.ai](https://app.instantly.ai). In the left menu, click **Email Accounts**, then click **\+ Add Email Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Custom SMTP">
    In the provider list, scroll down and select **Other SMTP** or **Custom SMTP**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter SMTP credentials">
    Fill in the fields with your mySMTP details:

    | Field | Value |
    | --- | --- |
    | SMTP Username | Your mySMTP username |
    | SMTP Password | Your mySMTP password |
    | SMTP Host | `relayxx.mysmtp.com` (from setup email) |
    | SMTP Port | `587` |
    | Encryption | `TLS` |
    | From Name | Your name or brand |
    | From Email | `you@yourdomain.com` (must match your SPF/DKIM domain) |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test email">
    Click **Send Test**. If the connection succeeds, you will see a green confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the account">
    Click **Save & Continue**. The account is now ready for campaigns in Instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## DNS and deliverability

Make sure your sending domain has the correct TXT records to reach the inbox:

| Record | Value |
| --- | --- |
| SPF | `v=spf1 include:spf1.mysmtp.com ~all` |
| DKIM | Set up in your [mySMTP dashboard](https://dashboard.mysmtp.com) |
| DMARC | Start with `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com` |

<Tip>
  Validate your DNS records with the [One-Click SPF / DKIM / DMARC Checker](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/) or build a DMARC record with the [DMARC Wizard](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/) in the mySMTP Deliverability Toolbox.
</Tip>

## Warm-up tips

Before launching large campaigns, warm up the mailbox to build sender reputation:

- Start with 10 to 20 emails per day.
- Gradually increase volume over 2 to 4 weeks.
- Mix in replies and engagement.

You can also use Instantly's built-in warm-up automation if it is available on your plan.

<Tip>
  Generate a day-by-day schedule with the [Warmup Plan Generator](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/#warmup-generator) in the mySMTP Deliverability Toolbox.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="SMTP connection fails">
    Confirm the host matches your setup email exactly, the port is 587, and TLS is selected. If your network blocks 587, try port 2525.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Authentication error (535)">
    Re-enter your mySMTP username and password. Make sure there are no extra spaces.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Emails land in spam">
    Verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are set up for the domain in your From Email. See [DNS settings quick explainer](/getting-started/dns-settings-quick-explainer) and [Improve email deliverability](/deliverability/improve-email-deliverability).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For more error codes, see [Common SMTP errors](/troubleshooting/common-smtp-errors).
