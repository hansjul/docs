mySMTP webhooks will notify your application in real time when something happens to an email you sent, such as a delivery, bounce, or spam complaint. Webhooks are planned for release in **late Q4 2026**, together with the [Email API](/api/index).

<Info>
  Webhooks are not available yet. Event types, payload formats, and setup steps will be published here at launch.
</Info>

## What webhooks are for

Typical uses for email webhooks include:

- Removing hard-bounced addresses from your lists automatically.
- Suppressing recipients who mark your mail as spam.
- Updating delivery status in your CRM or application.
- Alerting your team when bounce or complaint rates rise.
