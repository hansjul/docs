<Note>

## What to include

To resolve your issue faster, include:

- Your mySMTP account email
- A clear description of the problem
- Any error messages or codes
- SMTP logs or message headers when relevant

## Status page

Before contacting us about a potential outage, check [https://status.mysmtp.com](https://status.mysmtp.com) for known incidents and maintenance announcements.
