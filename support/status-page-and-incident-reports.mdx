## What the status page shows

Visit [https://status.mysmtp.com/](https://status.mysmtp.com/) to check current service health and incident status.

- Current service health for mySMTP infrastructure
- Active incidents and their status (investigating, identified, monitoring, resolved)
- Scheduled maintenance windows
- Historical uptime data
