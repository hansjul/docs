## What is the CAN-SPAM Act?

The CAN-SPAM Act (2003) regulates commercial email in the United States. It applies to any email with a marketing or promotional component, not just bulk sends.

## Key requirements

| Requirement | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Accurate headers | The From, To, and Reply-To fields must identify the real sender |
| Honest subject lines | Subject lines must reflect the actual message content |
| Ad disclosure | Clearly label the message as an advertisement when required |
| Physical address | Include a valid postal address in every message |
| Opt-out mechanism | Provide a clear unsubscribe link; honor requests within 10 business days |
| Monitor third parties | You remain responsible for compliance even when using an external sender |

## Best practices beyond the legal minimum

- Use confirmed opt-in (double opt-in) when collecting email addresses
- Keep your list clean by removing hard bounces and long-term inactive contacts
- Avoid spammy formatting such as excessive caps, too many links, or misleading offers
- Maintain a spam complaint rate under 0.3% to protect inbox placement

## One-click unsubscribe

For bulk marketing email, include a one-click unsubscribe link that complies with RFC 8058. This is now expected by major inbox providers and improves both compliance and deliverability.

## Other countries and regions

| Region | Law | Key feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canada | CASL | Requires express opt-in (consent) before sending |
| EU | GDPR + ePrivacy | Strict rules around data collection and consent |
| Australia | Spam Act | Requires consent and a working opt-out option |

<Warning>
  If you send to recipients in the EU, CAN-SPAM alone is not enough. You must also comply with GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive, including lawful basis for processing and clear consent records.
</Warning>

## Quick compliance checklist

- [ ] Accurate sender info in every message
- [ ] Honest subject lines
- [ ] Clear ad disclosure where applicable
- [ ] Physical postal address included
- [ ] Easy unsubscribe link present
- [ ] Opt-out requests processed within 10 business days
- [ ] Third-party senders monitored and vetted
