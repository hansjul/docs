## How to report

Submit your report through our dedicated form: [Report spam](https://mysmtp.com/report-spam)

Include as much detail as possible:

- Full message headers
- The exact email address the message was sent to
- The date and time you received it
- A description of why you believe it is spam or abusive

## What happens next

1. We review the report and investigate the sending account
2. If we confirm a violation, we take corrective action, which may include suspension
3. We may contact you if we need additional information

For other security or compliance questions, see [CAN-SPAM & Email Compliance](/security-compliance/can-spam-and-email-compliance) or [GDPR & Data Protection](/security-compliance/gdpr-and-data-protection).
