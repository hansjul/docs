## How we collect and process data

We only collect information essential for email delivery, including:

- Email addresses and SMTP credentials
- Usage logs and delivery status data

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising. All processing is based on legitimate interest and user consent principles.

## Where and how we store data

| Measure | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Secure, GDPR-compliant servers in the EU. The SMTP infrastructure uses no external US cloud services ([what this means for the CLOUD Act](/getting-started/introduction-to-the-service#what-is-the-us-cloud-act)) |
| Encryption | TLS 1.2+ for data in transit |
| Access control | Strict, role-based access for authorized personnel only |

## Data retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as necessary for service delivery and legal obligations. You can request deletion at any time under your right to erasure (Article 17). Contact [support@mysmtp.com](mailto:support@mysmtp.com) and we will confirm completion within 30 days.

## Your GDPR rights

| Right | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Request details on the data we store about you |
| Rectification | Update incorrect or incomplete information |
| Erasure | Request permanent deletion of your personal data |
| Portability | Receive a copy of your data in a structured format |

## Security practices

- Two-factor authentication (2FA) for account security
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
- Logging and monitoring to detect unauthorized access

<Tip>
  If you need to analyze headers or authentication data for GDPR-related checks, try the [mySMTP Email Header Analyzer](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/). It runs entirely in your browser, so your data is not sent to mySMTP servers.
</Tip>

For GDPR-related inquiries or data requests, contact us at [support@mysmtp.com](mailto:support@mysmtp.com).

If you need a Data Processing Agreement for GDPR Article 28 compliance, see [Data Processing Agreement](/security-compliance/data-processing-agreement).
