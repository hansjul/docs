Use the manual setup form when your company cannot pay online by credit card, for example when invoices must be paid by bank transfer or through an EAN number (Denmark).

## Order manually

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download and fill in the form">
    Choose the English or Danish version and complete all fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Bank as payment method">
    Tick **Bank** under **Payment method** (Danish form: **Betalingsmetode**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign and send the form">
    Date and sign the form, then email it to [support@mysmtp.com](mailto:support@mysmtp.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your setup email">
    When your order is processed, you receive a setup email with your SMTP hostname, login details and DNS instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Form fields

| Field | English form | Danish form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company | Company | Virksomhed |
| Tax or invoicing ID | VAT no. (if inside EU) | EAN nummer |
| Contact person and phone | Contact person, Phone | Navn kontaktperson, Tlf. |
| Address | Address, Postal code, City | Adresse, Postnummer, By |
| Email and website | Email, Website | Email, Website |
| Package | What package | Hvilken pakke |
| Sending domains | Sending domain(s) | Sende domæne(r) |
| Complaints address | Email for feedback loops / complaints | Not included |
| Billing period | Not included | SMTP ordre type: Månedlig or 1 år (-25%) |
| Payment method | Credit card, PayPal, Bank, Crypto | Credit card, PayPal, Bank, Crypto |
| Date and signature | Date, Signature | Dato, Underskrift |

<Tip>
  List every domain you plan to send from under **Sending domain(s)**. Check their SPF, DKIM and DMARC records first with the free [mySMTP Deliverability Toolbox](https://toolbox.smtp.ai/).
</Tip>
