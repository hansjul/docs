## Right of cancellation

You have **14 days** from the time the agreement is entered into to withdraw and request a refund.

| Condition | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Cancellation period | 14 days from agreement date |
| How to cancel | Email [support@mysmtp.com](mailto:support@mysmtp.com) |
| When the right expires | Once you start using the services |

<Warning>
  The right of cancellation and refund **no longer applies** if you make use of the services. Once you begin sending emails or actively configure your account, the 14-day cooling-off period ends.
</Warning>

## How to request a refund

To exercise your right of cancellation, notify mySMTP by emailing [support@mysmtp.com](mailto:support@mysmtp.com) within the 14-day window. Include your account details and the reason for cancellation.
